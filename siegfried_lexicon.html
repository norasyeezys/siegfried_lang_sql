swimman

/'swim:an/

infinitiveswimman

past 3plswummun
past partswommen
to swim
OHGswimmanOEswimmanONsvimja
used in
Āl swimmit in rīne
The eel swims in the Rhine.
PGmc *swimmaną. Strong verb class III, nasal+consonant subtype. Infinitive identical across OE, OD, OHG (all swimman). Ablaut series i-a-u-o: present stem i (swimmit), past sg a-grade (swam), past pl u-grade (swummun), past participle o-grade (swommen). The past 2sg uses the plural u-grade stem (swummi) — class III peculiarity. Present plural endings use -amēs/-at/-ant rather than later OHG -emēs/-et/-ent.

PGmc *winnaną. Strong verb class III, nasal+consonant subtype. Infinitive identical across OE, OD, OHG (all winnan). Ablaut series i-a-u-u: present stem i (winnit), past sg a-grade (wann), past pl u-grade (wunnun), past participle u-grade (wunnen — geminate nn blocks lowering to o, unlike swommen). Original meaning is strive/struggle/toil/labor-for; the English semantic shift to "win" (achieve victory) is a late Old English development. Gothic winnan attests the related sense of "suffer" (cf. Gothic wunns "passion").
